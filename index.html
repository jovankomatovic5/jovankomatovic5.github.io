About

Fault-tolerant distributed computing

Jovan Komatovic

Senior Researcher at Category Labs

My research focuses on designing robust and efficient distributed algorithms and understanding their fundamental limits.

I received my PhD in Computer Science from EPFL in 2025, advised by Rachid Guerraoui. My thesis, Optimal Byzantine Agreement with Little Cryptography, received the 2026 Principles of Distributed Computing Doctoral Dissertation Award.
